# مجتمع القرآن

An open-source, non-profit Quran app for daily reading, listening and reflection. Arabic only. The Quran is its only source.

> Work in progress. Nothing is released yet; see [the plan](docs/plan.md).

## What it will do

- **المصحف:** the printed Madani pages, with recitation that highlights the ayah and word being recited, search, and your reading position.
- **الحزب:** read and listen to the Quran hizb by hizb, at your own pace.
- **الأبواب:** doors of verses on one theme each, every quote shown inside its full verse.
- **الضياء:** a journey through food in the Quran, in 11 stations.
- **القمر:** tonight's moon, its rise and set, and the Quranic times of tasbih, computed on your phone for where you are.
- **المجتمع:** share a verse, ask, answer with a verse, and reflect, with every post reviewed before it appears.

## Principles

- Every verse comes from the Quran text, in full, never cut.
- No hadith, tafsir or people cited as authorities.
- No streaks, no guilt, no exclamation marks.
- Privacy: usage data only if you agree, never sold or shared (ADR 0008).
